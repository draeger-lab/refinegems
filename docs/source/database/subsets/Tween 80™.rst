Culture medium additive Tween 80™ (Tween 80™, oleate source)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
The culture medium additive Tween 80™ is mainly an oleate source but consists of a mixture of different fatty acids and 
partial esters of those.
